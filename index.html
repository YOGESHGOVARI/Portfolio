A little about myself

I am Yogesh Radhesh Govari, an Information Technology graduate and Software Developer from India. I completed my Bachelor of Engineering in Information Technology with a CGPA of 8.26 and earned Honours in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

During my engineering studies I developed an interest in programming, software development and AI. Subjects such as Mathematics, Deep Learning, Data Science, Cybersecurity and the Internet of Things helped me understand different areas of computing.

My interest in AI grew through academic projects in computer vision and machine learning. I enjoyed seeing how algorithms apply to practical problems rather than studying them only in theory.

One of my most meaningful projects was Visual Assist, an AI-powered assistive system that helps visually impaired people identify objects around them. It showed me how technology can address accessibility challenges.

After my degree I joined TCS as a Software Developer, where I've worked on frontend development, complex backend systems, database-driven applications, API integrations and AI agents for task automation. I enjoy learning new technologies, trying different approaches and continuously improving.

What drives me

I'm interested in the intersection of software engineering and AI. Traditional development taught me how applications are designed and maintained; working with AI agents opened up new possibilities in automation and intelligent systems.

My goal: keep growing as a developer while building deeper expertise in AI, Machine Learning and advanced software engineering.

01 — Visual Assist

AI-powered assistive technology for visually impaired individuals

Visual Assist helps visually impaired people identify objects in their surroundings — especially objects that look similar or are positioned around them. Our team built it with Convolutional Neural Networks and YOLOv5: it processes visual input, identifies the objects and gives audio feedback describing each object's name and position.

  • AI-based object detection
  • CNN and YOLOv5 integration
  • Object identification
